# ICA Motion Language v1

## Purpose

Motion is a communication tool. It is used to guide attention, reveal structure, reinforce proof, and return focus to the speaker.

## Motion behaviours

- Soft lift: introduces a message without interrupting the speaker.
- Side reveal: supports comparison and directional change.
- Scale settle: gives a number or CTA a controlled landing.
- Draw and rise: explains progress and growth.
- Staggered reveal: exposes process steps in the order they are spoken.
- Quiet focus: supports stories and emotional statements with minimal movement.

## Sound discipline

- A maximum of three cues per video.
- Cues must be at least 8.5 seconds apart.
- Cues are limited to proof, growth, process, and CTA moments.
- The speaker's voice remains dominant.
- No sound effect is added merely because a visual appears.

## Prohibited behaviour

- Random bouncing
- Continuous movement
- Repeated whooshes
- Decorative transitions
- Motion competing with captions
- Motion and camera punch-ins at the same moment
