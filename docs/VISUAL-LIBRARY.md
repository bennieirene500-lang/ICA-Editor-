# ICA Visual Library v2

The library remains internally generated and requires no member-supplied images or stock assets.

## Families and motion

1. Message cards — lift, side reveal, or quiet focus
2. Quote cards — quiet focus with restrained scale settling
3. Number cards — count-up and proof landing
4. Growth visuals — bars draw upward in sequence
5. Comparison cards — opposing side reveals
6. Process cards — ordered staggered steps
7. CTA cards — controlled settle and underline sweep

## Guardrails

- Visuals remain above caption-safe zones.
- Visuals do not overlap camera punch-ins.
- Visual density is outcome- and duration-aware.
- The speaker remains the hero.
- Motion intensity is reduced for story content.
