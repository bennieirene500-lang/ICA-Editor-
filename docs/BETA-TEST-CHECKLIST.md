# Founder and five-member beta test

## Founder test

1. Sign in.
2. Confirm the top bar shows five videos left.
3. Upload the golden test recording.
4. Choose the same outcome used in Milestone 9.
5. Confirm the final preview loads from Render.
6. Download the captioned video.
7. Press **Prepare Without Captions**.
8. Confirm the second version is produced without another transcription.
9. Confirm the allowance decreases by one, not two.
10. Produce a deliberately invalid video and confirm a failed job refunds the allowance.

## Beta expansion

Invite one member first. After a successful complete test, expand to five members.

Track:

- upload success
- production success
- time from upload to preview
- OpenAI spend
- Render stability and cold-start experience
- errors by device/browser
- whether members understand “videos left this month”

## Approval gate

Do not invite more members until:

- authentication works on phone and desktop
- one member cannot access another member’s output URL
- failures refund allowances
- temporary files disappear after expiry
- the full approved flow remains effortless
